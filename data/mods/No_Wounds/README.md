# No Wounds

Turns off the limb wounds CDDA added in late October 2026 (experimental 2026-10-25 and around it): with this mod,
a hit never gives a body part a scratch, cut, laceration, bruise or other wound with its own pain, healing time and
limb penalties. Damage, bleeding and everything else work as before.

How: it sets the game's `WOUND_CHANCE` option (the chance that damage creates a wound) to 0.

- Off by default. Tick it in the mod list when you make a world; it can also be added to an existing world.
- Wounds you already have heal as usual.
- Items and powers that add a wound on purpose (the Locutus regeneration serum, some Mind over Matter powers) still do.

Part of Xbro10's fork of Cataclysm: Dark Days Ahead.
