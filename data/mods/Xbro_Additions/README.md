# Additions

Additions to Cataclysm: Dark Days Ahead for the regular world. It doesn't need the Backrooms mods, and it is
on by default in new worlds on Xbro10's fork (you can turn it off in the world's mod list).

## What's in it

Items: 4 (Flamero-Mado spear, Specter shock lance, and a schematic for each). Recipes: 2.

### The Flamero-Mado spear

A rare end-game spear with a fuel tank in its shaft.

- Fill it with kerosene or gasoline and use it to light the head. Lit, every hit burns as well as pierces, and
  about one hit in three sets the target on fire. It gives off a little light, hisses, and burns about 25 minutes
  on a full tank before going out by itself. Use it again to put it out (the fuel stays in the tank).
- Unlit, it is still a better spear than the steel one.
- **Where to find it:** museum weapon displays and mansion weapon collections, very rarely.
- **Crafting:** Fabrication 8, Mechanics 6, welding and metalworking, and only after reading its schematic, which
  usually turns up with the spear.
- Sprites for UltiCa and UndeadPeople: on the ground (unlit and lit), its schematic, and in your hands (upright in
  UltiCa, held across the body in UndeadPeople).

With **Backrooms Expanded** it also turns up in the Backrooms: Level 2 tunnel crew stores, the Exchange's rare find,
and the Redeemer's remains; and Backrooms firesalt fuels it.

## Install

It ships with Xbro10's fork builds. For another build, copy the `Xbro_Additions` folder into the game's `data/mods`
(or your user `mods` folder) and tick **Additions** in the world's mod list.

### The Specter (shock lance)

The Flamero-Mado's sibling: a rare end-game spear that runs on a medium battery instead of fuel.

- Switch it on: every hit adds electric damage, about one hit in three stuns the target for a turn or two, and it
  gives off a faint blue light. It draws 25 W, so a full battery lasts a good while, and it switches itself off when
  the battery runs flat. Things immune to electricity (the Redeemer, robots) shrug it off.
- Switched off, it's a fine spear.
- **Where to find it:** lab science stores, military gear, and police and prison armouries, very rarely.
- **Crafting:** Electronics 7, Fabrication 6 and soldering, and only after reading its schematic.
- Sprites for UltiCa and UndeadPeople, on the ground and in your hands, with animated arcs when it's on.

With **Backrooms Expanded** it also turns up in Level 1 security office gun safes and as the Exchange's rare find.
