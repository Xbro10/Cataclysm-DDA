# Changelog

All notable changes to this mod are listed here, newest first.
Each release gets a dated entry with Added / Changed / Fixed / Removed sections.

## Unreleased

### Added
- The **Specter**, a shock lance and the Flamero-Mado's sibling: a rare end-game spear that runs on a medium battery. Switched on,
  every hit adds electric damage, about one hit in three stuns, and forked blue arcs crackle and lash out from its head in a soft glow (animated, on the
  ground and in your hands, UltiCa and UndeadPeople). Found in labs, military gear, and police and prison armouries; crafted only
  from its schematic (Electronics 7, Fabrication 6).
- The mod itself: additions to the regular world, no Backrooms needed, on by default in new worlds.
- The **Flamero-Mado spear** (moved here from Backrooms Expanded, same item ids, so existing saves keep theirs): a rare
  end-game spear with a fuel tank in its shaft. Fill it with kerosene or gasoline and light it: every hit burns as
  well as pierces, about one hit in three sets the target on fire, and a full tank burns about 25 minutes. Found in
  museum displays and mansion weapon collections; crafted only from its schematic. Sprites for UltiCa and
  UndeadPeople, on the ground and in your hands.
- The lit Flamero-Mado spear's flames are animated: they sway and flicker and embers rise, on the ground and in your
  hands, in UltiCa and UndeadPeople (8 frames looping about every half second).

### Fixed

- The Flamero-Mado spear only sets things on fire while it's lit. After putting it out, its fire chance could stay on your hits for a while; the hit now checks that the spear is burning.
