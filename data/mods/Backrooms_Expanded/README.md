# Backrooms Expanded

An add-on for **The Backrooms** mod for Cataclysm: Dark Days Ahead: new
levels, structures and survival resources (water sources, vending machines,
almond water).

**Status:** in development. Level 1 (the Parking Deck) is playable, reached through rare rippling walls on Level 0.  The way back is much harder to find. See `CHANGELOG.md`.

## Install

1. Copy the `Backrooms_Expanded` folder into your game's `data/mods/` (or
   your user folder's `mods/`).
2. When creating a world, enable **The Backrooms** (ships with the game)
   and then **Backrooms Expanded**.
3. Pick a scenario when creating your character: **Backrooms** starts on
   Level 0. To start on Level 1, pick one of:
   - **Wrong Exit**: alone, next to your stalled car.
   - **Breakroom Hire**: inside the Breakroom, among people who trust you.
   - **One of the Lost**: in a squatter camp. Harder; the Breakroom is wary of you.

## Requirements

- Current CDDA experimental build.
- The Backrooms mod (included with the game).
- Made with the UltiCa tileset in mind; every new ID has a `looks_like` fallback.
