# Changelog

All notable changes to this mod are listed here, newest first.
Each release gets a dated entry with Added / Changed / Fixed / Removed sections.

## Unreleased

### Added
- Breakroom standing. Every finished Breakroom job counts. **3 jobs:** Dot gives you the Breakroom supply key, which opens a new supply locker in the Breakroom stores for a weekly ration (almond water, food, first aid, sometimes a battery or light), and you get a "Breakroom regular" morale boost when you hand in jobs. **6 jobs:** Marcus gives you his hand-drawn map (copy it onto yours to reveal nearby lobbies, camps and a generator room), Dot gives you three bottles of almond water from her own tin, and the camp trusts you more. Ask Dot "Where do I stand with you lot?" to see where you are.
- Security office gun safe: the guard office now has a lockable gun safe (pick or pry it). About 2 in 5 hold a duty sidearm (Glock 19, M1911 or a .38 revolver) with a magazine and a little ammo; some hold only loose rounds.
- Oil-stained notes: a rarer note with a hand-drawn sketch. Activate it on Level 1 and it marks a generator room on your map, 3 to 8 squares away (on squares you haven't seen yet), with its own amber map sign. Half the time someone left a can of gas there too. Each note leads somewhere once. The old crumpled note about the generator now says the writer never found it again.
- Level 0 map sprites (UltiCa and UndeadPeople): the yellow rooms seen from above (three layouts of walls, doorways and lights), the border wall, the stairs (a staircase with an up or down arrow) and a signboard for the rippling-wall room.
- Level 0 furniture and item sprites (UltiCa and UndeadPeople): the water cooler (white cabinet, blue jug), the waycircle (a boulder with a painted glyph, candles and a bone), the Level 0 rippling wall (wallpaper whose stripes bend like cloth), the Level 1 sealed concrete patch, and almond water and its bottle (milky water, paper label).
- Level 0 wall and floor sprites (UltiCa and UndeadPeople): yellow wallpaper walls and grey walls that join properly at corners and junctions, showing their papered face only on the side you look at, damp yellow carpet with three stain variants, carpet and tile under buzzing ceiling-light panels, and faded tile. Level 0 used to borrow the base game's yellow wall and carpet.
- Entity sprites for the Smiler (a shadow with a glowing grin), Hound (a pale person on all fours), Deathmoth (a giant moth with eyespots) and Wretch (a skinless, raw-red figure with glowing yellow eyes, showing ribs and curved bone claws; its description now matches). Original pixel art in the style of UndeadPeople, used with UltiCa or UndeadPeople; other tilesets keep the old look-alike sprites.
- Level 2 map art, notes and graffiti. In UltiCa the tunnels (two variants), service ladders, boiler rooms, valve junctions, flooded dips and Tunnel Crew camps each have their own overmap sprite. Sweat-stained notes turn up at the landing, in junk heaps and in boiler lockers, and paint on the walls points the way (not always truthfully).
- Level 2 spawns and loot. Rats in the pipes, Hound packs in the corridors, Smilers in the long dark stretches, Deathmoths at the caged bulbs (sometimes already waiting at one), a few sluggish zombies, and later cockroaches and Wretches. Heaps of junk along the corridors hold pipe, fittings, scrap, tools and the odd battery; food is rarer than on Level 1.
- The Tunnel Crew: Level 2's only people. 1 to 3 camps per map in old pump rooms (bunks, a cold boiler they cook on, a drip pool for water, stores) with 2 to 4 crew who talk, trade and can be recruited. They explain the tunnels and the BLEED wheels and pass on rumours of a way further down. They offer no jobs; only the Breakroom does.
- Level 2 structure variants. Boiler rooms can be **cold** (dead boilers, someone camped there; cooler, with their stores and a note) or a **pump room** (two boilers, banks of pumps and its own BLEED wheel). Valve junctions can be a **burst main** (six joints hissing and a body short of the wheel) or a **control closet** (the wheel in a side room). Flooded dips can hold a **drowned traveller** with their gear, or be **draining** (just a pool at the crossing).
- Level 2 places. **Boiler rooms** (3 to 6 per map): a room of roaring boilers, the hottest spot on the level, with lockers of tools, batteries and fuel. **Valve junctions** (4 to 8 per map): turn the BLEED wheel (slow and loud) to shut every leaking steam joint within 20 tiles for 2 days. **Flooded dips** (2 to 4 per map): the tunnels stand in warm, knee-deep water, with sunken things to fish out.
- Level 2 heat and water. The tunnels run hot all year (about 30 C, rarely below 20 C, spikes past 40 C) with close, wet air, so you sweat, get thirsty and can get heat stroke. Leaking steam joints make the air around them 10 to 20 C hotter still. Condensation drips into pools in the tunnels (never run dry, boil before drinking).
- Level 2, Pipe Dreams (first step): a new dimension of hot, narrow maintenance tunnels, 3 tiles wide between pipe-choked walls, laid out as a maze of loops and dead ends that runs on across map squares, lit only by the odd caged bulb. **Way in:** about 1 Level 1 stairwell in 4 is still open; take the stairs down and they collapse behind you. **Way back:** rare service ladders (2 to 4 per map) climb to a floor hatch on Level 1. Followers within 20 tiles come along both ways. Heat, steam, places, people and spawns come in the next steps.
- Siphonable cars on Level 1: about 1 parked car in 4 still has a little fuel left (a few litres), enough to siphon for a generator or two. The rest are bone dry.
- Breakroom jobs. Ask Dot "Need a hand with anything?" or Marcus "Got any work for me?". **Dot**: *Fuel run* (bring 5 litres of gasoline, repeatable every couple of days), *Thin the Hounds* (kill 3 Hounds anywhere, repeatable), *The Hound den* (a den of four Hounds marked on your map; clear it), *Who they were* (bring back the roster from an overrun camp). **Marcus**: *Supply drop* (a padlocked cage with a sealed Breakroom crate; bring it back unopened), *Ride the lifts* (three elevator rides that end in a lobby; he reveals two more lobbies, repeatable). Rewards are supplies, almond water and Breakroom standing.
- Elevator lobbies on Level 1 (3 to 6 per map): a walled, brightly lit lobby with benches, a lost-and-found bin that refills about once a week, and sometimes a traveller waiting for the lift. Nothing spawns inside and Smilers won't enter the light, so they make good places to camp. Each elevator has a panel: **any floor** (a random lobby 5 to 30 squares away, marked on your map), **back** (the lobby you came from) and **home** (a lobby you've marked). Followers within 6 tiles ride with you, and every arrival chimes loud enough for nearby things to hear. Every ride is a small gamble, with a pop-up saying what happened: most are normal, but you can get the **wrong floor** (a dead zone with no way back), get **stuck** (an hour lost, thirsty, tired and shaken), have **company** (a Smiler in the car or a Hound pack drawn by the chime), find a **lucky floor** (a sealed storeroom nobody has looted), open on a **dark lobby** (power dead, blood, something still inside) or, about 1 ride in 50, find the **way out** to Level 0. Lobbies have their own map sprite in UltiCa, and a couple of new notes mention them.
- Level 1 useful places. **Security office**: a locked room with dead CCTV, the guard's lockers (flashlight, baton, batteries) and their logbook. **Generator rooms**: pour in two litres of gasoline and every dead ceiling light within 12 tiles comes back on for a week; sometimes a can of gas was left behind. **Storage cages**: padlocked chain-link lockups with the best tool loot on the level.
- Level 1 story structures. **Dead zones**: 2x2 patches of garage where every light has died; more Smilers and Hounds, more abandoned cars, and sometimes an old camp's stash worth the risk. **Overrun camps**: a squatter camp that didn't make it, with blood, a body, their gear and a last note, and sometimes a Wretch still there. **The exit ramp that isn't**: EXIT signs and a ramp up into a fresh brick wall with days tallied on it. **Graffiti**: spray-painted arrows and warnings from earlier travellers, some helpful, some not. Dead zones and overrun camps have their own map sprites in UltiCa.
- Notes on Level 1. Most arrival alcoves have a torn note left by someone who came before: one or two hints, never the whole picture, and not all of them true. Crumpled notes with bits of other people's stories turn up in lockers, junk and camp stores.
- Level 1 entities, all weaker than a zombie, with no grabs and packs of 3 at most so fights stay short. **Smiler**: a grin you only see up close in the dark; it flees bright light and keeps to the edge of yours. **Hound**: packs of 2-3 that hunt by sound and scatter when one dies. **Deathmoth**: weak swarms that gather at working lights (sometimes waiting at one). **Wretch** (day 30+): a survivor who stayed too long; slow, clumsy, and drops their old gear. Level 1 now spawns fewer rats and zombies to make room.
- Level 1 map art for UltiCa: the parking deck (three variants, some with parked cars or dead lights), arrival alcoves (a cyan rippling wall), the Breakroom (walled, with a green door) and squatter camps (a sandbag ring around a brazier) now each have their own overmap sprite. Other tilesets keep the old look.
- Three scenarios that start on Level 1. **Wrong Exit**: you start in an arrival alcove with your out-of-gas car in the lane outside, plus a flashlight and some road-trip food. **Breakroom Hire** (1 point): you start inside the Breakroom next to the Foreman, the Breakroom likes and trusts you, and the nearest squatter camp is marked on your map. **One of the Lost** (-1 point): you start inside a squatter camp with its survivors, the Lost like you, and the Breakroom is cooler towards you. Upstream's two Backrooms scenarios are still available.
- Almond water on Level 1: rare in attendant booths, camp stores and about 1 in 10 drinks machines, and Dot gives newcomers their first bottle. Drinking it now also calms you (a gentle mood boost for about 4 hours). This extends the Backrooms mod's own almond water rather than replacing it.
- Water on Level 1: drip pools under leaking pipes and inside every squatter camp (never run dry; boil before drinking), and two rigged taps of clean water in the Breakroom. NPCs within 6 tiles drink from them on their own.
- Vending alcoves: one or two stocked vending machines (snacks and drinks) under a working light. Pay with a cash card or break in.
- The Breakroom: a walled survivor hub on Level 1 with Dot Kowalczyk ("the Foreman"), who explains Level 1, and Marcus "Mile Marker" Reyes, who keeps the maps, can mark the nearest camp for you, and has rumours of what lies beyond. It forms once per world, 4 to 10 squares from your first arrival, and is marked on your map.
- Squatter camps: 4 to 8 sandbagged camps per map with 2 to 4 random lost survivors who can be talked to, traded with and recruited, each with their own story of falling through.
- Two factions: The Breakroom and The Lost. Each owns its camp's stores, so taking from them counts as stealing.
- Level 1, the Parking Deck: a new dimension of endless concrete parking garage (pillars, faded bay lines, ceiling lights that may be dead).
- Level 1 variety: puddles, leaking pipe runs, junk piles, guaranteed working lights, maintenance and electrical rooms, an attendant booth (best odds of food and drink), sealed stairwells with misleading signs, and the odd wrecked car.
- Level 1 loot (tools, car parts, hardware, batteries; food and water are rare) and monsters (mostly rats, some zombies; cockroaches after day 45).
- Rippling walls: a rare room on the ground floor of Level 0 (3 to 6 per map) whose back wall you can step through into Level 1. Followers within 5 tiles come along.
- Level 1 arrival alcoves. The patch of wall you fell through is sealed concrete: there is no going back the way you came. The only way back to Level 0 is a rare elevator ride.
- Mod skeleton: modinfo.json, README and this changelog.

### Changed
- Breakroom job rewards fit the job better: Thin the Hounds and Who they were also pay first aid, Supply drop also pays tools, and battery rewards are a few loose cells instead of whole boxes.
- Loot is now a themed handful instead of a whole store. Every crate, locker, junk heap and shelf on Levels 1 and 2 picks **one** kind of thing (tools, hardware, car stuff, lights, batteries, first aid, food or snacks) and holds 3 to 6 of it. Before, some piles pulled in the base game's full toolbox, battery box or vending-machine sets and could hold dozens of items.
- Redrew the Level 1 and Level 2 map sprites in an UndeadPeople style: textured ground for the garage, dead zones and tunnels, and landmarks shown as signboards with a pictogram (a rippling wall for arrival alcoves, a steaming mug for the Breakroom, sandbags and fire for squatter camps, bloodied sandbags for overrun camps, elevator doors for lobbies, a ladder, a boiler, a valve wheel, a water drop and a hard hat for the Tunnel Crew).
- The Level 1 and Level 2 map sprites now also show with the UndeadPeople tileset, not just UltiCa.
- Level 2 has far fewer leaking steam joints: about 1 tunnel square in 4 has one (it was about one per square), so the tunnels are hot but not scalding everywhere.
- UltiCa fallback sprites for almond water and its bottle, and overmap sprites for Level 1 squares (garage), the Breakroom (shelter) and the Level 0 rippling-wall room (an ordinary Level 0 chamber).
- You now land where you are (always at ground level on Level 1) when crossing between Level 0 and Level 1. You land just inside the nearest rippling wall within 30 tiles (including the room you came in through); only if there is none does a new alcove or thin-walled room form around you. Leaving Level 1 brings you out under where you were, not where you went in.

### Fixed
- Level 1 structures no longer count towards the main world's limit on required overmap specials. The elevator lobbies would have pushed the main world over that limit.
- Cars can no longer block an arrival alcove: any car touching the alcove or the 3 tiles in front of its opening is removed (the parking bays elsewhere keep all six car spots).
- Followers now arrive with you through rippling walls (they were left stranded where you first landed). Followers within 20 tiles come along and land on free tiles next to you.
